# 2. Technical Architecture & Assets

**2.1. A high-performance backend, an internal economy**

Solaris runs on a **high-performance centralized backend** that hosts the entire game state: planets, fleets, buildings, research, resources and Solar. This choice guarantees a smooth, responsive experience with **no transaction fees** for the player, whatever the size of the universe.

Solaris assets are **game objects**, managed by the server:

* **The Solar (SLR)**: the **internal currency** of the universe. It powers the whole economy (planet minting, boosts, advisors, secondary market) and exists only within the game.
* **Planets**: **unique game objects**, genuinely owned by the player and tradable on the built-in market. Each one is a distinct item in the collection, yet remains a game asset (neither a token nor an asset tradable outside the game).

**2.2. Access, payments and exit**

Players top up their Solar balance through the game's official **access ramp**, then spend it freely across every action in the universe. **Exit** happens through the **built-in market**: anyone can resell their planets and game assets to other players, in Solar. The economy stays **entirely internal** to Solaris, with no reliance on a third-party marketplace.
