# 4.2 Phase 2 — Forge

> **Goal:** Turn the working game into a visually immersive, intuitive experience. This phase is dedicated to the interface, graphic design, visual identity and optimization of the player experience.

#### 2.1 Visual identity & Design System

* Finalization of the Solaris visual identity (colors, typography, iconography)
* Creation of the full Design System (reusable UI components)
* Logo, favicon, splash screen and brand assets
* Consistent space palette: dark mode by default, neon accents

#### 2.2 Main Interface

* Empire dashboard: overview of all planets and resources
* Interactive galaxy view: navigable `[G:SS:PP]` map
* Solar system view: animated orbits and planet selection
* Planet view: management of buildings, construction queues, ships
* Combat interface: animated visual report
* Research interface: interactive, visual technology tree
* Internal messaging and notifications

#### 2.3 Graphics & Assets

* Planet illustrations (generated by orbital position and temperature)
* Ship sprites and animations (all 13 types)
* Icons for buildings, defenses, technologies and resources
* Combat animations (shots, explosions, debris fields)
* Dynamic space backdrops (nebulae, stars)
* Visual effects: jump gate, sensor phalanx, moon formation

#### 2.4 UX & Accessibility

* Optimization of critical flows (construction, fleet dispatch, combat)
* Interactive tutorial for new players
* Push notification system (incoming attacks, construction complete, etc.)
* Responsive mobile mode
* Internationalization (FR / EN minimum)
* Performance optimization (loading, animations, WebGL if needed)

#### 2.5 Communication & Public Presence

* Public Solaris landing page (teaser, features, whitelist)
* Trailer / gameplay presentation video
* Press kit and assets for partners
* Launch of the closed alpha version (beta testers)
