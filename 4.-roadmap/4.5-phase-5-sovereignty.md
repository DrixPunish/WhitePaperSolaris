# 4.5 Phase 5 — Sovereignty

> **Goal:** Progressively hand control over the key decisions of the Solaris universe to the community.

#### 5.1 Community governance

* A governance system where Solar holders weigh in on decisions
* Proposal system: above a Solar threshold, a player can submit a proposal
* Votes on: universe parameters, new mechanics, treasury allocations
* Empire Treasury transparent and publicly tracked

#### 5.2 Universe Governance

* Players vote on the parameters of future universes (speed, economy, duration)
* Creation of themed universes decided by the community
* Account blacklist / whitelist by community vote (anti-cheat)

#### 5.3 Ecosystem & SDK

* Public SDK to let third-party developers build tools for Solaris
* Public game-data API (stats, rankings, markets)
* Grant program for ecosystem contributors
* Partnerships with game communities and guilds

#### 5.4 Mobile Expansion

* Native iOS and Android app
* Native push notifications (attacks, construction, espionage)
* Account, balance and Voucher management directly in the app

#### 5.5 Durability & Resilience

* Strengthening of infrastructure resilience (redundancy, high availability)
* Backups and service continuity
* Continuity plan: the game keeps running even if the founding team steps away
