# 5.3 Empire Management

A player's empire is the set of celestial bodies under their control and the infrastructure they have developed.

#### Home planet & colonization

Each player starts with a randomly assigned **home planet**. It can neither be abandoned nor destroyed. Progression lets you colonize up to **8 additional planets** via Genesis Spaceships and the **Solarian Mapping** research.

* Each **colony** is a unique game object tied to the player's account
* The planet's size (number of buildable fields) is determined by its orbital position and a verifiable random factor
* A **Geoform Engine** can enlarge a planet by spending resources and Solar (SLR)

#### Buildings

Three building categories structure the development of each planet:

| Category            | Buildings                                                                          | Role                                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Producers**       | Iron Mine, Silica Mine, NovaGas Well, Solaris Center                              | Generate resources and energy continuously                 |
| **Storage**         | Iron Stock, Silica Silo, NovaGas Tank                                             | Increase capacity to avoid saturation                      |
| **Infrastructure**  | Automata Foundry, Atomic Workshop, Orbital Dock, Techno Lab, Geoform Engine       | Reduce construction times, enable R&D and expansion        |

> ⚠️ **Warning**
>
> **Geometric growth**: The cost of each upgrade follows exponential growth. A level-N building costs about 2× level N-1. Resources not stored beyond warehouse capacity are **permanently lost**.

#### Imperial Council

The **Imperial Council** gathers four **advisors** you **rent in Solar** (weekly) for permanent bonuses. The Solar paid is a **sink** (pure spending), never a source. Full details: [5.19 Imperial Council](5.19-conseil-imperial.md).

| Advisor               | Bonus                                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| ⚓ **The Admiral**     | +1 fleet slot · +1 expedition slot                  |
| 📐 **The Architect**  | Queue: buildings **and** research                   |
| ⛏️ **The Prospector** | +10% mine production · +5% expedition gains         |
| 🔧 **The Engineer**   | +10% energy · −25% research time                    |

> ✅ Owning **all four** advisors unlocks a **full Council** bonus (+1 fleet slot, +2% mines, +1% expeditions). The bonuses **stack with those of your House**.
