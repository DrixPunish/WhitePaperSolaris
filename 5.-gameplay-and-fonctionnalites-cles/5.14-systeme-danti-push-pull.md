# 5.14 Anti-Push/Pull System

To prevent unbalanced resource transfers between players, a strong account "inflating" a weak one (_push_), or the reverse (_pull_), Solaris continuously tracks the **trade balance** of every pair of players.

### Principle

For each **pair of players**, the **signed net** of transferred resources is accumulated, weighted by their relative value:

> **Balance = | net(Iron) × 1 + net(Silica) × 2 + net(NovaGas) × 3 |**

Balanced round trips cancel out: only the **net imbalance** counts.

### Limit & penalty

* **Allowed limit** = points of the **weaker player of the pair** × 5.
* As long as the balance stays **under the limit**, no problem.
* If it **exceeds the limit**, a **24 h alert** opens (non-resettable timer).
* If, at the deadline, the balance is **still above the limit** → **penalty**: mine production for **both** players drops to **0%**, for a duration proportional to the overshoot.
* Going back **under the limit** before the deadline immediately closes the alert, with no penalty.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> The system does not block trades, it **penalizes persistent imbalance**. Two players of comparable level can trade extensively; it is the massive one-way transfer to a much weaker account that triggers the penalty.
