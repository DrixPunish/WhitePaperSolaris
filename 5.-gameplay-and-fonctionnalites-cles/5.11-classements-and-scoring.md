# 5.11 Rankings & Scoring

Solaris scoring is computed server-side and viewable in real time.

> **Core rule:** `1 point = 1,000 resources invested` (buildings, research, ships, defenses). Stored, unspent resources generate no points; this system drives continuous investment.

| Ranking            | Criterion                                     | Period           |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| 🏆 **Overall**     | Total points of the empire                    | Permanent        |
| 🏗️ **Economic**   | Points of production buildings only           | Permanent        |
| ⚔️ **Military**    | Points of ships and defenses built            | Permanent        |
| 🔬 **Research**    | Points of the technology tree                 | Permanent        |
| 🤝 **Alliance**    | Sum of the points of active members           | Permanent        |

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> **Seasonal rewards**: the best players of each season receive **Solar** and **exclusive game items**: ship skins, alliance emblems, legendary planets with unique attributes.
