# 5.19 Imperial Council

The **Imperial Council** gathers four **advisors** you **recruit in Solar** to strengthen your empire. Each brings a permanent bonus inspired by the great officers of space strategy, production, fleets, expeditions, research, and the full quartet unlocks an extra bonus. It is an investment choice: **Solar spent** for an empire that runs faster.

> ℹ️ **Status**
>
> The Imperial Council is **deployed and active**. The bonuses apply **from the moment of rental** and **stack with those of your [House](5.9-classes-de-joueur.md)**. In-game access: **Profile → Imperial Council**.

> ✅ **A Solar (SLR) sink**
>
> Advisors are rented **weekly, in Solar**: the Solar paid is **removed from the game** (pure spending). It is one of the economy's **sinks**, never a source. See [3. Getting SLR](../3.-obtention-de-slr.md).

---

## 🏛️ The four advisors

| Advisor | Bonus | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **The Admiral** | +1 fleet slot · +1 expedition slot | **1,250 Solar / week** |
| **The Architect** | Queue: buildings **and** research | **1,500 Solar / week** |
| **The Prospector** | +10% mine production · +5% expedition gains | **1,250 Solar / week** |
| **The Engineer** | +10% energy · −25% research time | **1,000 Solar / week** |

### ⚓ The Admiral

The master of fleets. He adds **a fleet slot** (one more simultaneous mission) **and an expedition slot**: enough to project farther, on more fronts at once.

### 📐 The Architect

The engineer of megastructures. He unlocks the **construction queue** (see below): instead of launching a single build at a time, you **stack up to 5 orders** per queue, which chain automatically.

### ⛏️ The Prospector

The geologist of the void. He raises your **mine production by +10%** (Iron, Silica, NovaGas) and **expedition gains by +5%**: a background economic engine, on all your planets.

### 🔧 The Engineer

The master of reactors. He brings **+10% energy** (enough to power higher-level mines) and **−25% on the duration of all your research**: the technological accelerator.

---

## ✨ Full Council bonus

Owning **all four advisors at once** unlocks a set bonus:

> ✅ **Full Council**
>
> **+1 fleet slot · +2% mine production · +1% expedition gains**, on top of the individual bonuses.

---

## 📜 The rental

- Each advisor is rented for **1, 2 or 4 weeks**, paid in advance in **Solar**.
- An already-active advisor is **extended** (the remaining time is added to the new duration).
- On expiry, the bonus simply disappears, just rent again.
- The bonuses are **cumulative with your House**: a Prospector (+10% mines) and a House Solaris add up.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> The effects are **computed server-side** and visible everywhere, slots, production, research time. The **Statistics** screen even separates the **Council** share from the **House** share in the production breakdown.

---

## 🏗️ Building one at a time (the foundation)

The Council builds on a core Solaris rule, in the manner of the great space strategy games: **you build only one thing at a time per category.**

- **Buildings**: **a single build per planet** at a time.
- **Research**: **a single research across the whole empire** at a time.
- **Shipyard** (ships **and** defenses): **a single sequential queue per planet**: orders chain one after another, never in parallel.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> This rule applies **to everyone**, with or without an advisor. The **Solar rush** finishes the current item **and immediately launches the next** in the queue.

---

## 🗂️ The Architect's queue

Without the Architect, launching a building (or a research) while another is in progress is **impossible**: you have to wait. With **the Architect**, you **queue up**:

- Up to **5 pending items** per queue (one buildings queue per planet, one research queue for the empire).
- **Resources are only debited at the start** of each item, exactly like OGame: as long as an item waits its turn, its cost **stays in the planet's stock**. You can therefore queue without yet having the means to pay; the queue will wait.
- When a build ends, **the next one starts on its own**: its cost is checked then deducted at that moment. If funds are missing when it should start, the queue simply **waits** until you can pay (nothing is lost).
- **Cancellation**: removing a **pending** item is free, it had not cost anything yet (so nothing to refund).

> ⚠️ **Warning**
>
> Since the resources of a pending queue stay in your stock, they are **plunderable**: the queue is not a safe. You cannot "hide" your resources by stacking them in the queue, then cancel to get everything back.

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> Automation is what saves real time: you prepare a queue of several mine levels or a chain of research, and the empire progresses on its own between two logins.
