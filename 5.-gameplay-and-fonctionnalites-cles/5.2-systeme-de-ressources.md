# 5.2 Resource System

The Solaris economy rests on three primary production resources and two special resources.&#x20;

### Primary resources

#### **⚙️ Iron**

**Basic construction metal**

* The most abundant resource, used to build buildings, ships and defenses
* Produced by **Iron Mines**
* Relative reference value: `3 Iron = 2 Silica = 1 NovaGas`

***

#### **💎 Silica**

**Rare industrial ores**

* Essential for advanced technology research and onboard electronic components
* Produced by **Silica Mines**
* An intermediate resource, rarer than Iron, less strategic than NovaGas

***

#### **🔥 NovaGas**&#x20;

**Universal fuel**

* The most strategic resource; every fleet consumes it proportionally to distance
* Used for fusion plants and some critical research
* Produced by **NovaGas Wells**
* Production asymmetry: cold planets (positions 13–15) produce more of it

#### Special resources

| Resource          | Type                 | Description                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Energy (NRG)**  | Non-transferable     | Produced locally. A deficit reduces mine production proportionally to the shortfall                                                                                          |
| **Solar (SLR)**   | Internal currency    | Obtained through the access ramp, tournaments, expeditions or the market. Lets you activate officers, unlock slots and vote on universe parameters. Deflationary (burns + recycling) |

### Resource economy

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> **Strictly in-game emission**: Iron, Silica and NovaGas are only emitted through mine production. No arbitrary mint is possible. Resources consumed in construction or research are **burned**.
