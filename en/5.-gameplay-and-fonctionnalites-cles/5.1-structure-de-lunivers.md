# 5.1 Universe Structure

The Solaris universe is a persistent map. Its topology inherits from OGame's principles, augmented with modern mechanics: unique locations, real planet ownership, and programmed scarcity of strategic zones.

### Spatial hierarchy

The universe is organized into three nested levels, each defining a tier of distance, cost and strategic risk.

#### **🌌 Galaxies**

**1 to 5 galaxies per universe**

* The number of Galaxies varies with the number of Total Players / Active Players / Number of Planets
* The largest spatial unit; crossing a galaxy is a long, NovaGas-costly journey
* The topology is **circular**: the last galaxy is adjacent to galaxy 1, eliminating any dead edge
* Each galaxy holds **20 solar systems**

***

### **☀️ Solar Systems**

**20 systems per galaxy**

* Each system is organized around a central star with **15 orbital positions**
* The position determines a planet's temperature, size and production potential
* **Position 16** is reserved for deep-space expeditions

***

### **🪐 Planets & Moons**

**15 positions per system**

* Each location is **cryptographically unique**
* Planets are **unique game objects**, proof of colonization specific to each player
* Moons form randomly after large space battles
* Coordinates follow the `[Galaxy:System:Position]` format, e.g. `[1:18:12]`

### Orbital position effects

A planet's position within its system directly determines its production parameters. This system creates real trade-offs between profitability, safety and accessibility.
