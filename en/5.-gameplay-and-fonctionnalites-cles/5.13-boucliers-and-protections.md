# 5.12 Shields & Protections

Solaris offers several protections against attacks: an **automatic** protection for beginners, a **point-gap** limit between the attacker and their target, and a **premium shield** activatable on demand.

### 🛡️ Noob Shield (beginner protection)

Any player below **100 points** is automatically protected: they **cannot be attacked and cannot attack anyone**. A welcome message warns each new player that they will lose this protection once they cross 100 points.

* **Espionage remains possible** on a player under Noob Shield (you can prepare, not strike).
* The protection is **automatic**: nothing to activate.
* The Noob Shield disappears automatically once the 100th point is earned.

### ⚖️ Point gap (protection of the weaker players)

Beyond the Noob Shield, a player **cannot attack a target too weak** relative to them. The **maximum allowed point ratio depends on the target's points** (the defender): **the smaller the target, the more protected it is**.

| Target's points                            | Max ratio                           | The attacker cannot exceed                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _<mark style="color:$info;">0 – 100</mark>_ | <mark style="color:$info;">—</mark> | _<mark style="color:$info;">untouchable (Noob Shield)</mark>_ |
| 101 – 5,000                                 | **1 : 2**                           | 2× the target's points                                        |
| 5,001 – 50,000                              | **1 : 3.5**                         | 3.5× the target's points                                      |
| **> 50,000**                                | **1 : 5**                           | 5× the target's points                                        |

_Example:_ a target with **3,000** points (tier 1:2) can only be attacked by a player with at most **6,000** points; a target with **60,000** points (tier 1:5) can be attacked by a player with up to **300,000** points.

* This limit is **lifted during an active alliance war** between the belligerents (see [5.16 Alliance War](5.16-guerre-dalliance.md)).
* As with the Noob Shield, **espionage remains possible** (you can prepare, not strike).

### ⚛️ Quantum Shield

The Quantum Shield is an **activatable** protection that makes a player's planets **unattackable** for its duration.

<table><thead><tr><th width="153">Parameter</th><th>Value</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Cost</strong></td><td>5,000 Solar (SLR)</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Duration</strong></td><td>24 hours</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Cooldown</strong></td><td>24 hours after expiry before you can buy another</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Exclusive</strong></td><td>Incompatible with an active production boost (one or the other)</td></tr></tbody></table>

* **Effect**: blocks **all** incoming attacks while active.
* **Espionage remains possible**: an attacker can keep preparing their assault for when the shield falls.
* **Visible in the Atlas**: a protected player's planets display a characteristic **aura**, so everyone knows they are unattackable without even having to spy on them.
* **Deactivates** if an attack is launched from the protected planet.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> The Quantum Shield does not interrupt an attack **already in flight** at activation time: it applies, then the shield protects against any new attack.
