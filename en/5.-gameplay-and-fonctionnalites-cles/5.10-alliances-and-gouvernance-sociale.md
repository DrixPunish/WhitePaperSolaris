# 5.10 Alliances & Social Governance

Alliances are **collective entities** that bring players together around a shared treasury, roles with distinct permissions and coordinated-war tools.

> ℹ️ **Status**
>
> Alliances are **fully functional**: creation, roles, treasury and boosts, alliance war and coordinated combat. Formalized diplomacy (treaties signed in-game) does not exist yet and may arrive later in the roadmap.

### How it works

* **Formation**: creating an alliance is **free** (choice of a name and a tag). It does **not** cost any Solar; the creator becomes its `Founder`.
* **Member roles**: `Founder`, `Officer`, `Member`, `Diplomat`; each role has distinct permissions (member management, diplomacy, war, treasury).
* **Alliance treasury**: members contribute resources to it. The treasury funds **alliance boosts**, starting with **increasing the number of seats** (see below); other boosts may be added.
* **Ranking**: an alliance's weight is the **sum of its members' points**.

### 👥 Maximum size

An alliance's number of seats is **dynamic**, indexed on the universe's population:

$$\text{Seats} = \max\big(3,\ \lfloor 0{,}15 \times \text{active players} \rfloor\big) + \text{boost seats}$$

* **Guaranteed minimum**: **3 seats**, even on a very sparsely populated universe.
* **Base**: **15%** of the universe's **active players** (those who own at least one planet), rounded **down**. Empty accounts do not count. The cap therefore **grows with the server**.
* **Boost seats**: the treasury can buy **extra seats** (up to **+10**), each at a rising price (the cost doubles at each tier, base 10,000 of each resource).

> 💡 **Note**
>
> Since the base follows the population, a small top alliance cannot "lock up" all the best players at once: server growth and the treasury remain the two levers to grow.

### ⚔️ War & diplomacy

An alliance's diplomatic life currently runs through its **conflicts**:

* **Alliance war**: in-game declaration, notice period, score, prestige and rewards (see [5.16](5.16-guerre-dalliance.md)).
* **Coordinated Combat (ACS)**: group attack and group defense between allies (see [5.17](5.17-combat-coordonne-acs.md)).
* **Inter-Galactic Portals**: alliance territories to conquer and defend with a garrison (see [5.18](5.18-portails-inter-galactiques.md)).
