# 5.4 Technology Research Tree

The **Techno Lab** is the engine of long-term progression. Each technology unlocks or improves key capabilities. Research is planetary but its benefit is global (the whole empire). _(A selection of key technologies below; the full tree has 16 technologies.)_

### 🔩 Core Technologies

| Technology                | Effect                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Strategic AI**          | +1 simultaneous fleet slot per level              |
| **Cosmic Scanner**        | Improves the depth of espionage reports           |
| **Pulse Reactor**       | Unlocks and improves intermediate ships           |
| **Ballistic Systems**     | +10% Attack of all ships per level                |
| **Deflector Fields**      | +10% Shield of all ships per level                |
| **Nano-Braided Alloys**     | +10% Hull of all ships per level                  |

### ⚡ Advanced Technologies

| Technology                  | Effect                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Quantum Drive**      | +30% speed of all fleets per level                                       |
| **Hyperdensity Nodes**      | +5% cargo capacity of all ships per level                                |
| **Neural Network**             | Merges lab power across several planets                                   |
| **Solarian Mapping**    | +1 colonization slot · simultaneous expeditions = `min(5 ; ⌊√level⌋)`     |

### 💀 Special Technologies

| Technology                        | Prerequisite                 | Effect                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Gravitational Manipulation**          | 300,000 units of energy      | Unlocks the **Astral Titan**     |
| **Plasma Overheating**               | Ballistic Systems Lv. 12     | +30% massive Attack bonus        |

> ⚠️ **Warning**
>
> **Gravitational Challenge**: **Gravitational Manipulation** requires generating **300,000 units of energy in a single request**. This feat is a collective meta-game challenge that may require coordinating an entire alliance.
