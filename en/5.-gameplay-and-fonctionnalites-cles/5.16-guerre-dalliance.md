# 5.16 Alliance War

**Alliance War** is the pillar of Solaris's **high-level PvP**. Declaring war on a rival alliance **lifts the anti-bashing protections** between the belligerents, **unlocks coordinated combat (ACS)** (see [5.17 Coordinated Combat (ACS)](5.17-combat-coordonne-acs.md)) and puts **Prestige** at stake, the military experience that levels your alliance up and unlocks permanent bonuses.

> ℹ️ **Status**
>
> The war system runs **server-side** and is being activated progressively. Its advanced formalization (declarations, treaties and surrenders recorded and viewable by all) is planned for later in the roadmap, continuing the alliance governance described in [5.10 Alliances & Social Governance](5.10-alliances-and-gouvernance-sociale.md).

> 💡 **Note: why a real war system**
>
> In classic space-conquest games, a "war" is just a forum thread with no mechanical effect, apart from manually lifting the attack limit. Solaris builds **on top** a full, native system: in-game declaration, live dashboard, destruction score, cumulative prestige and concrete rewards.

---

## ⚔️ Declaring war

The declaration is **non-refusable but announced**. The aggressor declares, a **24 h notice period** elapses, then the war activates: the target **cannot dodge it**, but has this delay to prepare, recruit and evacuate its most fragile members.

* **Who can declare**: the **Founder** or a **Diplomat** of the alliance.
* **Strength of an alliance**: the **sum of the points of all its members** (same metric as the [ranking](5.11-classements-and-scoring.md)).

### Guardrails at declaration

| Guardrail | Rule | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum size** | Both alliances ≥ **3 members** | No pretext war mounted against an isolated target. |
| **Framed strength gap** | Target valid if its strength ∈ **\[ my\_strength ÷ 2 , my\_strength × 5 ]** | Anti-crushing downward; "punch-up" (hitting stronger) is capped at **5×**. |
| **Re-declaration cooldown** | **7 days** before re-declaring on the same alliance | Anti-harassment of a single target. |
| **Simultaneous wars** | **2 maximum** per alliance, **all roles combined** | An alliance can fight B **and** C, but no more. |
| **Uniqueness per pair** | A single active or preparing war between two alliances | No duplicates to stack effects. |

> ⚠️ **Warning: the dogpile is blocked**
>
> The cap of 2 wars counts both **declared** and **endured** wars. An alliance already engaged in two conflicts **can no longer be targeted**: it is impossible to coalesce five alliances against a single victim.

## 🕒 Life cycle of a war

```
 (peace)
   │  Declaration (Founder / Diplomat) + guardrails
   ▼
 PREPARATION ──── 24 h notice ────►  ACTIVE ──────►  ENDED
   │  · aggressor frozen               │  · anti-bashing lifted      · winner designated
   │  · exit window open               │  · ACS unlocked              · prestige distributed
   │    for the target's small members │  · live score                · alliances thawed
   │  · cancelable by the aggressor    │  · memberships frozen
   ▼                                   ▼
 (back to peace)                      Surrender · Expiration · Ceasefire
```

* **Preparation**: lasts for the **notice period** (24 h by default). The aggressor can still **cancel** its declaration (which immediately thaws its alliance).
* **Active**: war effects apply (anti-bashing lifted, ACS, score).
* **Maximum duration**: an unresolved war automatically closes after **10 days**.

## 🚪 Alliance lock & exit window

During a war, **alliance movements are framed**: this is the pillar that prevents using departures/arrivals to **dodge** the conflict or **bypass** anti-bashing, while offering an **honest way out** for the smallest.

### Lock matrix

| | **AGGRESSOR alliance** | **TARGETED alliance** |
|---|---|---|
| **Preparation (24 h)** | Join ❌ · Leave ❌ | Join ✅ · Leave ⚠️ *(reserved for members below the threshold)* |
| **Active** | Join ❌ · Leave ❌ | Join ❌ · Leave ❌ |
| **Ended** | Free ✅ | Free ✅ |

* **The aggressor is frozen from the declaration**: it chose the moment, so it had all the time to prepare. It can neither recruit last-minute reinforcements nor let its members flee.
* **The target can recruit** during preparation: it endures the declaration, so it is entitled to reinforce its defense.
* **In active war, everything is frozen** on both sides: you do not leave an alliance at war.
* **Kicking follows the same rules as leaving**: otherwise a founder could kick members to make them dodge the war.
* **Dissolving an alliance at war = forfeit** (equivalent to a surrender), never a free escape.

### The exit window

At the **declaration**, a snapshot is frozen: the **average points of the aggressor's members**. During preparation, a member of the targeted alliance can leave **if and only if their points are below this average**.

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> This is the counter to anti-bashing circumvention. Without it, a large alliance could declare war on an alliance containing a few small players **solely** to lift their protections and farm them. Here, **these small members leave before the first shot**. Those who stay are above the aggressor's average: they are not anti-bashing victims, but fighters who **chose** the conflict.

All members of the target receive a **notification** at declaration (both values, enemy average and my points, are shown); the **"Leave"** button appears only for those below the threshold, with a **countdown** reminding the time left. A player who **joins** a targeted alliance during preparation must give **explicit consent** ("you will be frozen until the end of the conflict").

## ⚔️ Core effect — lifting anti-bashing

This is the mechanical heart of war. Between belligerents, the PvP brakes are released:

| Protection (in peace) | At war between belligerents |
|---|---|
| **Beginner shield** (< 100 pts) | **Kept**: a too-weak player is never dragged into a war. |
| **Strength-gap block** (target ≤ 50% of attacker) | **Lifted**: this is the very point of war. Protecting the weak is handled **upstream**, via the exit window. |
| **Aggressiveness limit** (attacks per 24 h) | **Lifted**: no more cap between belligerents. |
| **Quantum shield** / **Vacation Mode** | **Kept**: legitimate protections, never bypassable by war. |

The effect is **reciprocal**: from activation, **both** alliances can strike freely. Declaring also means exposing yourself.

> 💡 **Note: the only offensive lever at the top of the table**
>
> In peace, the strength-gap block protects the smaller: a top player no one can approach **can attack no one**. Alliance war is therefore **the only way** for the top of the ranking to lead a real offensive, which makes it the **heart of endgame PvP**.

## 🎯 War score

Each combat between belligerents feeds a live **dashboard**:

* **Destroyed points**: the value (Iron + Silica + NovaGas) of enemy units destroyed by each side, accumulated over the whole war.
* **Plunder points**: resources **plundered** from the enemy also count, on the **same scale** as destruction (rate: **1,000 resources plundered = 1 point**). They add to the attacker's score **even without combat**, for example when the opponent has withdrawn their fleet or left a planet undefended.
* **Contribution per member**: each player accumulates their own points (destruction + plunder); the **top contributors** of each side are shown, with the detail of resources plundered.
* **Extras**: fleets destroyed, debris generated.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> Counting plunder closes a classic loophole: **withdrawing your fleet** to leave nothing to destroy no longer keeps you safe, because the enemy who enters and plunders still scores points. Active defense therefore always beats fleeing.

### Exact per-shot attribution

In coordinated combat (ACS), **each destroyed unit is credited to the player who landed the blow**: whoever dealt the most damage to a target scores the point. It is therefore impossible to **steal the destruction points** of your allies by sending an empty fleet, the firepower actually committed alone makes the destruction score (plunder points, by contrast, go to whoever actually brings back the loot).

The **War** screen shows the two tags, a timer, the **score gauge** of both sides, the top contributors and the event feed. Since an alliance can wage up to two wars, the screen lists ongoing conflicts then opens the detail of each.

## 🎖️ Prestige & alliance levels

War rewards with **Prestige**: the alliance's **military experience**. The principle: **lots of desirability, zero economic inflation**. Prestige is not spent, it raises the **alliance's level**, which unlocks **automatic, free bonuses**.

> ✅ **Good to know: two merits, two currencies**
>
> * **Prestige = military merit** → alliance level → automatic bonuses.
> * **Treasury resources = economic merit** → purchasable boosts (see below and [5.10](5.10-alliances-and-gouvernance-sociale.md)).

### How Prestige is computed

Prestige works by **ratios**, not fixed tiers: a war **with no real combat earns almost nothing**, and a **win against someone stronger earns big**. The loser **loses** prestige, but less if they genuinely fought. Prestige is **cumulative** and can never drop below **0**.

* **The winner's gain** grows with the damage it dealt **and** with the strength gap against it (rewarding the "punch-up").
* **The loser's loss** is **softened by the effort** made: the more it destroyed, the less it loses.
* **Draw**: each side receives **25%** of what it would have earned as a winner, with no loss.

| Scenario | Winner gain | Loser loss |
|---|---|---|
| **Close war between equals** (significant real damage) | **+72** | −7 to −21 depending on effort |
| **Successful punch-up** (win against 5× stronger) | **+365** | up to −120 |
| **Fake war** (almost no destruction) | **+1.5** | −0.5 |
| **Honorable draw** | A **+18** / B **+12** | – |

> ⚠️ **Warning: no prestige farming**
>
> Since the gain is proportional to the **real destruction relative to the opponent's size**, chaining sham wars against complicit alliances earns **almost nothing**. Prestige rewards **real** battles.

### The level ladder

**Each level requires 100 XP more than the previous one.** The levels are **not locked**: losing prestige can **bring you back down** a level (and thus lose the associated bonus). Excess members stay, but the alliance can no longer recruit above its cap.

| Lv. | XP total | **Automatic bonus** | **Boost unlocked** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 100 | +1 member slot | – |
| **2** | 300 | +10% on treasury donations | – |
| **3** | 600 | +10% speed of intra-alliance flights | **Logistics Bridge** |
| **4** | 1,000 | −10% boost cost | – |
| **5** | 1,500 | +1 member slot | **Intensive Recovery** |
| **6** | 2,100 | −10% intra-alliance fuel | – |
| **7** | 2,800 | +0.2% production per member | **Mobilization** |
| **8** | 3,600 | +5% defense rebuild | – |
| **9** | 4,500 | +5% ship construction speed | **War Effort** |
| **10** | 5,500 | +1 fleet slot for **all** members | **War Banner** *(ultimate cosmetic)* |

Each tier also unlocks an evolving **banner frame**, displayed next to the alliance tag. Level 10 is a multi-year **prestige endgame**, consistent with a permanent server.

### 🔥 War boosts

On top of the free bonuses, the **alliance treasury** funds **repeatable temporary boosts**, each unlocked at a given level. These are **tactical tools** (a burst before an offensive), never a source of income: their price **always exceeds** the value they generate.

| Boost | Effect | Duration | Unlocked at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Logistics Bridge** | +25% speed of intra-alliance flights | 24 h | Level 3 |
| **Intensive Recovery** | +50% recycler collection capacity | 24 h | Level 5 |
| **Mobilization** | −25% ship construction time | 12 h | Level 7 |
| **War Effort** | +10% production for all members | 24 h | Level 9 |

## 🏁 Ending a war

A war can end in several ways:

* **Surrender**: the Founder of the side that gives up surrenders (possible after a minimum duration of 24 h); the other side is declared **winner**.
* **Expiration**: after 10 days, the side with the **highest score** wins. If the gap is too small, it is a **draw**.
* **Ceasefire**: one side **proposes** it, the other **accepts** → **draw**. The proposer can attach a **tribute** (Iron / Silica / NovaGas) paid from **its** treasury to the opponent: the struggling side "pays to stop". Atomic transfer, insufficient funds, proposal refused.
* **Dissolution**: dissolving an alliance at war equals a **forfeit**.

At closing, **memberships are thawed** on both sides and normal anti-bashing is restored.

> ✅ **Good to know: the memory of campaigns**
>
> Feats of arms are engraved for good: **permanent medals** on the profile ("Veteran of {war}", Top-3 contributor, Best destroyer), a **campaign title** displayable next to the username, **public war history**, a **trophy hall** (defeated tags) and the alliances' **prestige ranking**. Cumulative prestige, for its part, **never** resets.

---

## 🛡️ Anti-abuse guardrails

| Targeted exploit | Counter |
|---|---|
| **Fake war to farm your own weak members** | Framed strength gap + **exit window** (small members leave before the 1st shot) + prestige ≈ 0 if the gap is large + 7-day cooldown. |
| **Anti-bashing circumvention** | Only those **above the aggressor's average** remain; the beginner shield (< 100 pts) is **always** kept. |
| **Dodging the war** (leave / get kicked) | Memberships **frozen**; kicking follows the same rules as leaving; dissolution = forfeit. |
| **Last-minute reinforcements** | The aggressor **cannot recruit** from the declaration. |
| **Coalition (dogpile)** | Cap of 2 wars **including endured wars** → beyond that, the alliance can no longer be targeted. |
| **Ranking manipulation** (declare + flash surrender) | Minimum duration before surrender; prestige weighted by **real destruction**. |
| **Inflated score in group combat** | **Exact per-shot attribution**: impossible to score without firing. |

---

> 🔭 **War unlocks coordinated combat.** Once two alliances are at war, their members can launch **group attacks** and deploy **defensive garrisons** at their allies': this is **ACS**, detailed on the next page, [5.17 Coordinated Combat (ACS)](5.17-combat-coordonne-acs.md).
