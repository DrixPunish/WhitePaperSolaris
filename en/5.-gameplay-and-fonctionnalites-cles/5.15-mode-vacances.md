# 5.15 Vacation Mode

**Vacation Mode** lets you pause your account safely, without risking repeated attacks while you are away.

### Activation

The mode can only be activated if the account is **"at rest"**:

* no fleet in flight,
* no construction or research in progress,
* no production at the shipyard.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> It can still be activated **even under an incoming attack**: the already-launched attack applies normally (as with the Quantum Shield), but **no new attack** is possible afterward.

### Effects during vacation

* **Untouchable**: no fleet can be sent **to** or **from** a player on vacation (attack, espionage, transport…).
* **Frozen production**: mines produce **0%** for the entire duration of the mode: no resources accumulate, but no saturation either.

### Duration

* **Minimum duration: 48 h.** After this delay, the player can **exit manually** at any time.
