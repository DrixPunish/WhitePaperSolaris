# 5.6 Planetary Defenses

Defenses are **fixed** structures built on a planet or a moon. They consume no fuel and cannot move.

| Defense                            | Class         | Use                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Kinetic Turret**                 | Light         | Volleys of metal projectiles, mass defense               |
| **Impulse Cannon**                 | Light         | Energy bursts, rapid fire against small units            |
| **Beam Cannon**                    | Intermediate  | Continuous beam able to pierce medium armor              |
| **Ion Projector**                  | Heavy         | Ionized discharge that weakens enemy shields             |
| **Mass Driver**                    | Heavy         | Hyper-dense shells, devastating against heavy ships      |
| **Solaris Cannon**                 | Elite         | Superheated plasma, the most powerful defense            |
| **Small / Large Planetary Shield** | Shield        | Energy dome, massively absorbs damage                    |

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> **Post-combat rebuild**: In Solaris, defenses have a **70% chance of automatic rebuild** after a battle. This rate is improved via the **Engineer** advisor, creating an economic resilience absent from OGame.
