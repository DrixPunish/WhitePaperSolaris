# 5.20 Rarities & Asteroids

Every planet in the collection has a **rarity**: from the most common to the most prestigious. These rarities are not bought: they are **earned**, at the end of a long economic chain where players sacrifice their moons, hunt meteorites and fuse **asteroids**. Asteroids are the **engine** of this whole system.

> ℹ️ **Status**
>
> **The system is deployed and playable**: asteroid acquisition (moon sacrifice, meteorites, crafting), **rarity advantages**, **asteroid market** in Solar and the **Solar Generator** of Diamond planets. The **fusions** (raising a planet's rarity) are **in place** but their tiers will **open progressively**, during dedicated events. Asteroids are internal **game objects**: neither a currency nor an asset tradable outside the game.

---

## 💎 The rarity scale

Four tiers, from most common to rarest:

**Basalt → Ruby → Jade → Diamond**

The rarer a planet, the more prestigious (and, eventually, advantaged, see *Advantages* below). The pyramid is **guaranteed by the game**: a broad base of common planets is needed for a handful of Diamonds to exist. Bringing a single **Diamond** into being represents a colossal cascade, on the order of **14 sacrificed moons**, tens of thousands of Solar and hundreds of millions of resources.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> The **asteroids** themselves are capped at **Jade**: Diamond only exists at the end of the chain, on a **planet**.

---

## 🪨 Earning asteroids

Asteroids are not minted: they are **earned** through three paths.

### 🌑 Sacrifice your own moon

Deliberately destroying one of your [moons](5.7-lunes.md) (cost in **Solar**) gives **1 asteroid**, whose rarity is drawn by lottery:

| Rarity | Chance |
|---|---|
| **Basalt** | 98% |
| **Ruby** | 1.5% |
| **Jade** | 0.5% |

> ⚠️ **Warning**
>
> The sacrifice is **irreversible**: the moon **and all its lunar buildings** are lost. This is the great moon dilemma, **keeping** them for their abilities (Lunar Base, Shipyard, Phalanx, Gate) or **cashing them in** as an asteroid. *(It is also the only lottery in the system, along with the mint; all fusions, by contrast, are deterministic.)*

### ☄️ Meteorites

**Meteorites** are **account-bound, non-transferable** fragments. You get them in two ways:

- **Destroy an opponent's moon** with a **Titan Astral** → **2 to 6 meteorites**, depending on the size of the moon destroyed.
- **Rare expedition** in [Deep Space](5.8-expeditions.md) → **1 meteorite** (rare outcome).

**20 meteorites craft into 1 Basalt asteroid** (free craft). This is the **military and exploratory** path to rarity: no Solar, but enemy moons to shatter and expeditions to multiply.

---

## 🔗 Raising rarity (fusions)

Once you have asteroids in hand, two **deterministic** fusions let you climb the scale.

> ℹ️ **Status**: mechanic **in place**, tiers **opened progressively** (during dedicated events).

### Fusing asteroids

**2 asteroids** of one rarity + **Solar** → **1 asteroid of the next rarity** (Basalt → Ruby → Jade).

### Fusing a planet

This is the ultimate goal: **1 planet** + **2 asteroids of its rarity** + **Solar** → the **planet moves up one tier** (it keeps its coordinates, buildings and defenses).

To preserve the pyramid, each step up requires a **population floor** in the collection:

| Step up | Condition |
|---|---|
| → **Ruby** | at least **750** existing Basalt planets |
| → **Jade** | at least **300** existing Ruby planets |
| → **Diamond** | at least **90** existing Jade planets |

## 🎖️ Rarity advantages

Each planet gains **economic** bonuses based on its rarity. Basalt = neutral base; Ruby, Jade and Diamond ramp up:

| Advantage (per planet) | Ruby | Jade | Diamond |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mine production** (Iron / Silica / NovaGas) | +5% | +10% | +25% |
| **Energy production** | +10% | +20% | +45% |
| **Storage capacity** | +25% | +50% | +100% |
| **Construction time** | −5% | −10% | −15% |
| **Research time** | −1% | −2% | −5% |

- The **research time** bonus is **cumulative** across all your rare planets (research being global to the account), capped at −75%. The other bonuses apply **per planet**.
- **Diamond only**: unlocks a **Solar Generator**, a building that pays out a share of the **Empire Treasury** pro rata (never any Solar creation).

> 🔒 **Red line: zero pay-to-win**
>
> Rarity advantages **never** touch raw combat stats. Rarity is about **prestige and economic efficiency**, not bought military power.

---

## 🧭 An economy that holds together

Asteroids are **not** a currency and are **not** traded outside the game: they are **game objects**, tradable between players **in Solar** with a **floor price**. The whole chain (resources → fleet → battle → debris → moon → asteroid → rarity) ties the **real game** to value, which makes rarity an **engagement reward**, never a mere purchase.
