# 5.9 Houses

Each player can pledge allegiance to one of the **three Houses** of Solaris, or remain **Bannerless**. A House grants **permanent passive boosts** as long as you serve it: this is the Solaris equivalent of OGame classes, rooted in the lore of the three great factions of the Imperium.

The choice is made right after creating your username. **The first membership is free.** Changing House afterward has a cost (Solar + seniority) and leaves a temporary scar, the **Rebuke**.

### **🏳️ Bannerless**

No boost, no constraint. A player can refuse any allegiance at launch and join a House later; the first membership stays free.

## Choice & Change

* **First membership: free** (once only), offered right after choosing your username.
* **Free welcome change**: during a short **discovery window** after the first membership, you can **change once for free**, with no cost, no seniority delay and **no Rebuke**. A way to fix a first choice made too quickly, without penalizing yourself.
* **Change** (from Settings → House), once the welcome window has passed: **5,000 Solar** + a **seniority of at least 7 days** in the current House. Leaving a House to become Bannerless again follows the same rules.
* These conditions discourage opportunistic "House-hopping" (switching just for a war weekend) while leaving real freedom to reorient.

***

## The Rebuke

Leaving a House is never trivial: for **48 hours**, the deserter carries the **Rebuke** of their original House, a temporary penalty reflecting the signature boost they have just abandoned, along with a dedicated narrative warning.

| House left       | Rebuke penalty (48 h)                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| House Solaris    | −10% mine production, −5% energy                                                                                      |
| Nephilim         | +15% fuel, −1 level of combat research, −1 fleet slot, −20% ship speed                                                |
| Quantum Synod    | +10% research time, −1 expedition slot, Phalanx unusable, +3 points of black-hole risk on expeditions                |

The Rebuke **stacks** with the boosts of the new House: you can perfectly well serve the Nephilim while still carrying the Synod's Rebuke for two days.

## House Anchoring (coming soon)

Eventually, allegiance to a House will be **backed by the corresponding in-game ownership**, much like planets (see the Roadmap, Horizon Phase). During the current play and balancing phases, access to Houses stays **entirely free**, with no ownership condition. When this anchoring is activated, the rule will apply to everyone, existing members included.
