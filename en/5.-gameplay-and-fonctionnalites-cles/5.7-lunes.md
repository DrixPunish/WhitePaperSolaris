# 5.7 Moons

**Moons** are rare celestial bodies born from large space battles. A moon occupies the same `[Galaxy : System : Position]` location as its planet and unlocks unique strategic capabilities.

### Formation

After a battle, the debris field generated has a **1% to 20%** chance of spawning a moon, proportional to the **size of the debris field**, and therefore to the scale of the battle. Only one moon per planet.

### Lunar development

A moon starts bare and develops with its own buildings (buildable fields there are limited and grow slowly):

| Lunar building           | Role                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lunar Base**           | Foundation building, unlocks the moon's development                    |
| **Robotics Foundry**     | Reduces construction times on the moon                                 |
| **Orbital Port**         | Lets you build ships from the moon                                     |
| **Sensor Phalanx**       | Spies on enemy fleet movements at range (see below)                    |
| **Subspace Gate**        | Instant fleet jump between two moons (see below)                       |

> 💡 **Note**
>
> A new moon has **no resources**: you must **transport** Iron, Silica or NovaGas there from a planet before you can build.

### 🛰️ Sensor Phalanx

From a moon, the Phalanx **scans the fleet movements** of a targeted player (attacks, transports, returns) on planets within range. A major defensive intelligence tool: **see attacks coming** before they land, to prepare your defense or save your fleet.

### 🌀 Subspace Gate

The Gate links two moons of the same empire and allows an **instant fleet jump** from one to the other, **with no travel time or fuel consumption** (with a recharge time between jumps). A way to redeploy your forces across the universe in an instant.

### 💥 Destruction

A moon can be **destroyed** by a **Titan Astral** in a dedicated attack. Its destruction **frees the orbital slot** and removes the moon along with everything built on it, the only way to get rid of an opponent's moon (or your own).

> 💎 **Meteorites**: destroying a moon with the Titan Astral releases **meteorites** (a few units), the raw material of the rarity system, they are crafted into asteroids to raise planet rarity (see [5.20 Rarities & Asteroids](5.20-raretes-and-asteroides.md)).

> ℹ️ **Status**
>
> Like planets, moons are designed as **rare game objects**, genuinely owned by the player.
