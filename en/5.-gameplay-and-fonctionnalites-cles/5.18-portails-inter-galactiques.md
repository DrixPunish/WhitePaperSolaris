# 5.18 Inter-Galactic Portals

**Inter-Galactic Portals** are **highly strategic, contested points** in the Solaris universe: five colossal structures set **between the galaxies**, which alliances fight over to **tax all the traffic** crossing their borders. Holding a portal means levying a toll on every enemy fleet that passes, and paying the daily price of defending it.

***

## 🌀 The galactic ring & the 5 portals

The Solaris universe is a **ring of 5 galaxies** (`1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 – 1`). Between each pair of neighboring galaxies stands **one Portal**, so **5 portals** in total:

| Portal   | Links                |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| Portal 1 | Galaxy 1 ↔ Galaxy 2  |
| Portal 2 | Galaxy 2 ↔ Galaxy 3  |
| Portal 3 | Galaxy 3 ↔ Galaxy 4  |
| Portal 4 | Galaxy 4 ↔ Galaxy 5  |
| Portal 5 | Galaxy 5 ↔ Galaxy 1  |

Each portal is **held by a single alliance** at a time, or **neutral** (free). It is not a planet: it has **no economy** and never belongs to a player, only to an **alliance**.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> A portal is targeted via a **sentinel coordinate** specific to the border it sits on (much like Deep Space at position 16, or moons). The two neighboring galaxies point to **the same** portal.

***

## 🛰️ Access — the Galactic Ring

From the header of a galaxy view, the **🌀 Portals** button opens a full-screen view of **the Ring**: the 5 galaxies in a pentagon, with the portals between them. Each portal is marked with its **holder's badge** (emblem + alliance tag, or "Neutral"). A single glance is enough to see **who holds what**.

A tap on a portal opens its **dedicated screen**: a large central structure, the holder, the garrison present, any debris, and the available actions, **⚔️ Attack / Take**, **🛡️ Reinforce**, **♻️ Recycle**.

***

## ⚔️ Taking and holding a portal

Each fleet sent to a portal is a **capture attempt**, resolved **on arrival**: exactly like a [5.17 Coordinated Combat (ACS)](5.17-combat-coordonne-acs.md) battle. The contest is **permanent and continuous**.

The behavior on arrival depends on the portal's state **at the moment the fleet lands**:

* **Neutral portal** → **instant capture**. The fleet switches to **garrison** and the alliance becomes the holder.
* **Enemy portal** → **immediate ACS combat**: the incoming fleet (and its possible union, see below) faces the **garrison present**.
  * **The attacker wins** → the portal flips to its alliance, and its **survivors become the new garrison**.
  * **The defender holds** → the holder is kept.
* **Portal of your own alliance** → **reinforcement**: no combat, the fleet joins the garrison.

> 💡 **Defender's edge**
>
> **A draw benefits the holder.** Holding a portal means something: the assailant must _defeat_ the garrison, not merely match it.

### The garrison & the NovaGas surcharge

Sending a fleet to a portal means choosing a **garrison duration** (**from 1 h to 32 h**) and **prepaying in NovaGas**: the round-trip fuel **plus** a **surcharge** proportional to the fleet's size and the duration. At the end, the fleet **returns automatically**; an **early recall** is possible (with loss of the surcharge).

> ⚠️ **Warning: owning is not defending**
>
> A held portal **with no garrison** (all fleets returned or destroyed) stays with the alliance, but becomes **very vulnerable** to the next attacker. **Holding a portal requires a permanent presence**: that is the heart of the tension.

### Coordinated attack (ACS union)

Several allied fleets can form a **union** to strike the portal **together, to the second**, and crush the garrison in a single aggregated assault, the same mechanism as the [attack ACS](5.17-combat-coordonne-acs.md). Ideal for wresting away a well-defended portal.

***

## 💰 The toll — how a portal pays

Any fleet that **crosses a border whose portal is held by a rival alliance** pays a toll, **charged on departure**. It stacks **for each border crossed** (crossing two held portals = paying twice), and **intra-galaxy trips never pay anything**.

> ✅ **Good to know**
>
> The **members of the holding alliance** pay **no toll** on their own portals.
>
> **Espionage** fleets know how to slip discreetly past a portal and therefore pay **no tax**.

The toll has **two parts**:

### 1. Cargo tax (in resources) → alliance treasury

**1% of the cargo carried** (Iron, Silica, NovaGas) is taken and paid to the holder's **alliance treasury**. It depends on **ownership** of the portal: it applies **even if the garrison is empty**, and benefits **the whole alliance**.

### 2. Power toll (in Solar) → to the present defenders

**1% of the traveler's fleet power** is taken in **Solar** from their balance (scale **1 point = 1 Solar** → a fleet of **100,000 points** pays **1,000 Solar**), then **distributed immediately, pro rata**, among the **garrisoned fleets** at the time of passage.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> Only players **physically in garrison** receive the Solar, they risk their fleet to collect it. If the portal is held but **without a garrison**, the Solar toll is **ignored** (no one to pay), but the **resource tax still applies**. ⇒ **Holding a garrison is the condition for Solar income.**

### Summary before dispatching a fleet

On the fleet dispatch screen, if the route crosses a held portal, a **summary banner** appears **before confirmation**: for example "⚠️ Portal toll: **−1,000 Solar → \[Alliance]**". It shows live the cargo and Solar concerned by the tax.

### You never pass for free

If the traveler does not have enough Solar, the fleet simply **cannot leave its departure planet**.

***

## ♻️ Debris field — for everyone

Each portal combat generates a **debris field** (30% of the cost of destroyed ships) at the portal's coordinates. This **debris field is recyclable by anyone.** It is a strong economic incentive to prowl around the hottest portals.

***

## 🎯 The strategic tension

An Inter-Galactic Portal is never "won" for good: **taking it is easy, keeping it is a permanent war.**

* **Owning** brings **resources** to the treasury (passive income, even without a garrison).
* **Defending** brings **Solar** to the present fleets (active income, but you risk your fleet).
* **Attacking** costs NovaGas and a fleet, but a poorly guarded portal is a tempting prey.

This is the great endgame of alliances: controlling the crossroads of the galactic ring, and making the enemy pay for every border crossing.
