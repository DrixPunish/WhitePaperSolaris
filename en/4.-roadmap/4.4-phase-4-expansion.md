# 4.4 Phase 4 — Expansion

> **Goal:** Enrich the game's content, deepen the advanced mechanics and launch the first official competitive seasons.

#### 4.1 Advanced Gameplay Content

* Multiple universes with different parameters (speed ×2, ×5, accelerated economy)
* Temporary galactic events (rare-resource zones, PvE invasions)
* Advanced diplomacy system (federations, declared wars, trade treaties)
* Peer-to-peer galactic market for resources and ships between players
* Bounty system on targeted players

#### 4.2 Competitive Seasons

* Launch of the official Season 1 (set duration, e.g. 3 months)
* Seasonal rankings with rewards in Solar and exclusive game items
* Cosmetic rewards: ship skins, legendary planets, alliance emblems
* Hall of Fame: the top players of each season immortalized

#### 4.3 Partnership Program

* Partnerships with other games and communities
* Partnerships with content creators and gaming-focused streamers
* Community ambassador program paid in Solar
* Participation in gaming conferences&#x20;

#### 4.4 Whitepaper & Documentation

* Whitepaper v2.0 update incorporating community feedback
* Full technical documentation of the API and the economy
* Public game wiki (mechanics, guides, strategies)
* Public changelog and patch notes
