# 4.3 Phase 3 — Horizon

> **Goal:** Switch on the game's economic layer. Open the planet market, launch the Solar economy and deploy the internal economy (mint, burns, cyclic recycling).

#### 3.1 Economy & Access ramp

* Setup of the official access ramp (topping up the Solar balance)
* Solar balance per account, integrated into every in-game action
* Smooth buying and spending experience, with no technical friction for the player

#### 3.2 Planets & Moons

* Real ownership of colonized planets (attributes: position, size, temperature, history)
* Moons: created during post-combat formation
* Built-in secondary market: buying and selling planets between players, in Solar

#### 3.3 Officers

* The 5 officer types with variable rarity (Common → Legendary)
* Built-in P2P market for officers
* Officers owned in-game, replacing the subscription system

#### 3.4 Solar (SLR) — Launch

* Activation of the internal currency (deflationary mechanisms: burns + recycling)
* Official access ramp (Solar created on purchase) and built-in market
* In-game Solar integration: House anchoring, slot purchases, voting
* Cyclic redistribution (buffer → prize pools) to fund in-game gains

#### 3.5 Tokenomics — Finalization

* Finalization and publication of the full Whitepaper v1.0
* Public economy dashboard (circulation, burns, recycling, market volume)
