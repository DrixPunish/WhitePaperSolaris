# 4.1 Phase 1 — Genesis

> **Goal:** Lay the game's solid foundations. Implement all core gameplay mechanics in centralized mode (classic server, database) to validate the gameplay loop before switching on the economic layer.

### 1.1 Infrastructure & Architecture

* Choice and setup of the technical stack (backend, database, API)
* Architecture of the core entities: Universe, Galaxies, Systems, Planets
* Coordinate system `[G:SS:PP]` and procedural universe generation
* Data model: Player, Empire, Planet, Building, Fleet, Research
* Authentication and player accounts system
* Real-time server infrastructure (WebSocket for fleet movements)

### 1.2 Resource Mechanics

* Continuous production engine (Iron, Silica, NovaGas, Energy)
* Production calculation by mine level and orbital position
* Storage system with caps per warehouse level
* Saturation and loss of resources beyond storage
* Production differential by orbital position (positions 1–15)

### 1.3 Construction & Empire

* Construction queue per planet (1 building at a time)
* Progressive cost calculation (geometric growth per level)
* Time reduction via Automata Foundry and Atomic Workshop
* Construction slot system (unlockable)
* Colonization: Genesis Spaceship deployment, colony creation
* Geoform Engine: expansion of available fields
* Multi-planet management (up to 9 planets per player)

### 1.4 Research Tree

* Techno Lab with power calculation (multi-planet network)
* Implementation of the 15+ technologies in the full tree
* Dependencies and prerequisites between technologies
* Lab power shared across planets (Intergalactic Network)
* On-record tech score per player

### 1.5 Fleets & Movements

* Construction of the 13 ship types (stats, costs, prerequisites)
* Mission system: Attack, Spy, Colonize, Recycle, Transport, Station, Expedition, ACS
* Transit time calculation (speed × distance × NovaGas factor)
* Fuel consumption (NovaGas) proportional to distance
* Simultaneous fleet slots (unlockable via Computer Technology)
* Recall of fleets in transit

### 1.6 Combat Engine

* Round-based combat resolution (max 6 rounds)
* Random shot calculation weighted by Attack/Shield/Hull stats
* Rapid Fire (RF) system between ship types
* Generation of the detailed combat report
* Debris field calculation (30% IRON + SILICA of destroyed ships)
* Moon formation probability (1–20% depending on field size)
* Resource plunder (cargo limit)
* Automatic defense rebuilds (70% probability)

### 1.7 Planetary Defenses

* Construction of the 9 defense types
* Integration into the combat engine
* Interplanetary missiles and ABM
* Defense domes (damage absorption)

### 1.8 Espionage System

* Spy probes: information gathering (resources, buildings, defenses, fleet)
* Report depth based on the Espionage technology level gap
* Counter-espionage and detection

### 1.9 Moons

* Post-combat moon formation (probabilistic calculation)
* Moon features: Sensor Phalanx, Jump Gate
* Destruction via the Astral Titan

### 1.10 Rankings

* Real-time scoring: Overall, Economic, Military, Research
* Alliance ranking
* Ranking history and snapshots

### 1.11 Alliances

* Alliance creation, management and dissolution
* Member roles: Founder, Officer, Member, Diplomat
* Alliance chat and internal messaging
* Alliance forum and announcements
* ACS: coordination of fleets between members
* Diplomacy: non-aggression and allied statuses

### 1.12 Houses

* Implementation of the 3 Houses: House Solaris (Collector), Nephilim (General), Quantum Synod (Explorer)
* Application of House boosts on the mechanics (production, fleet, combat, research, expeditions)
* First membership free; paid change (Solar + seniority ≥ 7 days) with a 48 h Rebuke penalty

### 1.13 Officers

* Implementation of the 5 officers and their bonuses
* Slot-based equipment system
* Temporary mode (subscription) before the switch to in-game ownership

### 1.14 Expeditions

* Dispatch logic toward position 16
* Random reward table (resources, ships, nothing, danger)
* Bonus from the Quantum Synod House (extra expedition slot and gains)
