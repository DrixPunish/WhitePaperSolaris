# 6.8. Protection Against Abuse

Because Solar is an **internal currency** (not a token listed on an external market), protection does not focus on a "price" or a "listed supply", but on **in-game fairness**: preventing a player, a whale or a multi-account from cornering the economy or the rewards.

| **Mechanism**            | **Description**                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Anti-sybil**           | Detection of multi-accounts on rewards and earning mechanics (a single real beneficiary per account). |
| **Capped pools**         | Each earning mechanic draws from a finite pool that empties (see 6.5.8): a single player cannot drain the redistributed Solar indefinitely. |
| **Earning caps**         | Per-player, per-period limits on earning mechanics (e.g. expeditions/day quota), to smooth out distribution. |
| **Framed market**        | The built-in market (planets, resources ↔ Solar) applies indexed rates and a commission, limiting speculation and farming. |
| **Anti-bashing / anti-push** | Gameplay protections (point gap, anti-push/pull) preventing abusive value transfers between accounts. |
