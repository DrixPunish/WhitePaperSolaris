# 6.5. Outflow Mechanisms (Outflow / Burn)

**6.5.1. Minting a New Planet**

* **Floor price:** $30, i.e. 30,000 Solar.
* **Burn:** 100% of the mint's SLR is burned.

**6.5.2. In-App Market Fees**

* **Commission:** 6% on every market transaction (planet sale, resources-for-SLR exchange, etc.)
* **Destination:** the **entire 6%** feeds the **recycling buffer** (see 6.5.8), which then redistributes it to the pools. This commission is not burned: it is **fully recycled** back into the game economy.

**6.5.3. Challenge Entry**

> ℹ️ **Status — Coming soon** (later stage of the roadmap).

* **Entry ticket:** Variable by challenge (e.g. 10, 50, 100 SLR)
* **Destination:** 100% of the tickets are added to the challenge prize pool. At the end:
  * 80% distributed to the winners
  * 20% **burned** (imperial gaming tax)

**6.5.4. The Imperial Court (Tax Collector)**

> ℹ️ **Status — Coming soon** (mechanic in design).

* **Principle:** Players send resources (converted to SLR value) to the Court to try to become Tax Collector.
* **Destination:** 100% of the value sent is **burned** (the resources are removed from the game; the SLR is destroyed if sent in SLR).

**6.5.5. Rushes / Boosters**

* If a player pays SLR to speed up a construction/research: **burned**.

**6.5.6. Scrapyard (unit resale)**

* **Principle:** at the Merchant, a player resells the **stationed ships** and **defenses** of their planet for a fraction of the resources invested (direct credit in Iron / Silica / NovaGas).
* **Recovery rate:** **50%** by default, up to **80%**. The rate is chosen **at each resale** and costs SLR: 50%: 1,000 · 60%: 2,000 · 70%: 3,000 · 80%: 4,000 SLR. A **per-transaction** model: no tier is kept from one resale to the next.
* **Burn:** the SLR paid is **burned**. The resold units are **destroyed** and you never recover 100% of the investment; the Scrapyard stays a lossy conversion, never a source of gains.

**6.5.7. Peddler (buying resources)**

* **Principle:** at the Merchant, a player **buys resources with Solar** (capped production pack, 24 h window).
* **Solar spent:** subject to cyclic recycling (see 6.5.8), part is **burned**, the rest feeds the redistribution buffer.

**6.5.8. Recycling & Cyclic Redistribution**

This is the heart of the Solar cycle: spent Solar is not simply destroyed, part of it is **recycled** then **redistributed** to the mechanics that pay it out, without ever creating any.

* **Recycling tap (R):** when a player spends Solar in a sink, a fraction **R** (configurable) is **recycled** into a **recycling buffer**; the rest (**1 − R**) is **burned** (deflation + buyback demand). The rate **R** is tuned to the game phase (growth / stability / shrinkage of circulation).
* **Pools:** the buffer **pours out in cycles** (weekly, at a fixed hour) into **pools**, each tied to a mechanic that distributes Solar (Solar Generator, Expeditions, PvE…). The split between pools is **configurable**.
* **Self-regulation:** each mechanic **draws from its pool**; if it runs dry, it **stops distributing** (floor at 0) and players naturally shift to other mechanics. **No Solar is created**: redistribution only **recycles** the Solar already in circulation.
