# 6.2. Economic Philosophy

Our model rests on 4 core pillars:

1. **Simplicity**: A single currency avoids the confusion and FUD of dual-currency models.
2. **Sustainability**: Control of the Solar in circulation through numerous burn mechanics.
3. **Aligned interests**: Players, whales and developers all share the same goal.
4. **Programmed scarcity**: The less Solar remains in circulation, the more potential value each unit gains.
