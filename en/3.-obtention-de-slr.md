# 3. Getting SLR

### 3.1. Getting the Token

Players can acquire Solar (SLR) in several ways, combining external funding, competition, trading and free play. This variety ensures a dynamic ecosystem where every player profile has a place.

***

#### 3.1.1. External acquisition (access ramp)

The most direct way to get Solar: topping up your balance from outside the game.

* **Principle:** the player tops up their Solar balance through the **[official access ramp](https://www.solaris-imperium.com/marketplace)**, and the Solar is then **immediately available** for every in-game action (planet minting, boosters, market, etc.).
* **Benefits:** simple and transparent, with no technical friction for the player.

> 🔗 Buy Solar: [www.solaris-imperium.com/marketplace](https://www.solaris-imperium.com/marketplace)

***

#### 3.1.2. Weekly Challenge Rewards

> ℹ️ **Status — Coming soon**
>
> Feature planned for a **later stage** of the roadmap. Described here for reference; the details (categories, prize pools, tickets) may evolve before it ships.

Competitions are an engagement driver and a way to redistribute Solar to the best players.

* **Principle:** each week, several challenges run across different categories so that every player profile can shine.
* **Paid entry:** to take part, the player pays an entry ticket in Solar. This ticket is:
  * partly added to the challenge prize pool (80%);
  * partly burned (20%) to feed deflation.
*   **Challenge Categories (examples):**

    | Category               | Description                            | Frequency |
    | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------- |
    | **Supreme Emperor**    | Best overall score (total points)      | Weekly    |
    | **Warlord**            | Largest fleet destroyed                | Weekly    |
    | **Industrial Magnate** | Highest resource production            | Weekly    |
    | **Space Explorer**     | Most planets discovered (minted)       | Monthly   |
    | **Legendary Spy**      | Most successful espionage reports      | Weekly    |
* **Reward Distribution:** at the end of the challenge, the total prize pool is distributed to the best players in each category (e.g. Top 10, with 40% for first place, 25% for second, etc.).

***

#### 3.1.3. Selling Planets on the Secondary Market

Since planets are **unique game objects**, they can be traded between players.

* **Principle:** a player can list one or more of their planets on the built-in in-app market.
* **Price:** set freely by the seller in Solar.
* **Fees:** a commission is taken on every sale (burned share / Treasury).
* **Use cases:**
  * a player who quits can liquidate their empire;
  * a player can "specialize" a planet (e.g. a mining planet, a military planet) and resell it for more;
  * a player can buy an already-developed planet to progress faster.

***

#### 3.1.4. In-Game Resource Conversion (Peddler)

At the **Merchant**, the player converts their in-game resources into Solar through the **Peddler**, with no external funding and without creating inflation.

* **Principle:** the player **sells resources** (Iron, Silica, NovaGas) for Solar.
* **Mechanism (anti-farm, anti-inflation):**
  1. **Rate indexed on the rankings:** the conversion rate is indexed on the top players; the richer the economy gets, the more resources conversion costs, so farming neutralizes itself.
  2. **Cap per period:** a quota limits how much a player can convert over a given window.
  3. **Source of the Solar:** exclusively the **redistribution pools** (see 6.5.8). Empty pool = no more conversion. **No money creation.**
* **Effect:** an F2P lever (playing intensively lets you earn Solar) that stays **self-regulated**, the indexed rate discourages bots and smooths out the distribution.
