# 6.4. Inflow Mechanisms (Influx)

How does SLR enter the economy? Mainly through:

1. **Access ramp**: New players acquire Solar through the official access ramp and use it in-game.
2. **Challenge Rewards**: Tournament winners receive Solar from the "Game Rewards" reserve.
3. **Selling Planets**: A player who sells their planet (a game object) receives Solar from another player.
4. **In-game gains**: expeditions, Solar Generator (Diamond planet) and other PvE mechanics. These gains are **funded by the pools** of the cyclic redistribution (see 6.5.8): no Solar is created, only recycled.
5. **Peddler (Merchant)**: a player can **sell resources for Solar** (rate indexed on the rankings, capped to limit farming).
