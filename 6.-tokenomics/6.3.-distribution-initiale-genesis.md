# 6.3. Creation & Reserves of Solar

Solar is an **internal currency**: it is created on demand and exists only within the game. There is **no crypto token, no initial distribution to investors and no pre-mine**. Solar enters the economy only when a player acquires it through the official **access ramp**, then circulates entirely in-game.

| Source of Solar | Nature | Detail |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Access ramp** | Creation on demand | The player buys Solar through the official ramp. No pre-mined stock: Solar is created at the moment of purchase, at the current price. |
| **Recycling buffer** | Recycling (no creation) | Fed by a fraction of spending (see 6.5.8) and by the market commission. Redistributed in cycles to the prize pools. |
| **Redistribution pools** | Recycling | Every mechanic that earns Solar (Solar Generator, Expeditions, PvE…) draws from its pool. Nothing is created: the Solar is **recycled** from circulation. |

> **No hidden inflation:** the only point of Solar creation is the access ramp (purchase by the player). Everything else, in-game rewards included, comes from **recycling** Solar already in circulation, never from an arbitrary mint.
